## What Gate 3 asks, and the answer

*Does anything on a screen assert a figure nothing computed?* **No — on all nine.**

Every figure carries its own denominator on screen. The four distinctions the console exists to
draw are all rendered rather than assumed:

- **Absence apart from zero.** `— no feed recorded`, `— no time recorded`, `— the run recorded no
  repository`, and on the pull request `CI ATTEMPT 0` beside *"How many times the run has waited on
  CI"* — a real zero with its denominator, next to real absences, drawn differently.
- **Never-measured apart from nothing-here.** Signals: *"A role with nothing attached was never
  asked… which is a different fact from an attached integration that was asked and had nothing to
  report."* Vendor's traffic column reads `never measured`.
- **Staleness apart from liveness.** Workflow: *"A standing is the checkpoint's own answer —
  nothing here says a node is executing."* `CHECKPOINT SPAN 55s` explicitly refuses to be read as
  run duration, because a run waiting on the customer's CI writes no checkpoint.
- **Provenance at two levels.** The rung is a column on every call-site row and a field on the
  finding, and Detectors states plainly that *"the rung is a class of evidence, not a position on a
  good-to-bad scale, so no colour here grades anything."*

**The three refusals hold.** No composite score, no health figure, no traffic light, no green dot,
no liveness pulse on any of the nine. The Overview carries the `HEALTH SCORE POLICY` panel stating
the refusal, and Detectors — the screen most able to invent one — says outright that it *"is not a
leaderboard and carries no precision or accuracy figure… a ratio computed from open findings alone,
with no labelled corpus behind it, would measure nothing."*

Badges are the permitted kind throughout: recorded values from a closed vocabulary, legible without
their colour — `STATIC`, `warning`, `ATTACHED` / `NOT ATTACHED`, `3 OPEN FINDINGS`,
`READ-ONLY: TRACKED IN GIT`.

## Finding 1 — two screens report fleet scope from a workspace-scoped address

Reached at `/repositories/furever/vendors/…` and `/repositories/furever/detectors`, both report
that nothing selected a repository: Vendor says `REPOSITORY SCOPE: Nothing selected one on the way
here` and `FINDINGS COUNTED OVER: Every repository the index has seen`; Detectors says
`SCOPE: Every repository the index has seen` under a subtitle asking about *"this workspace's"*
false positives.

**This is not a Gate 3 failure and it must not be reported as one.** Both screens state their real
scope accurately and neither asserts a figure nothing computed — which is precisely what this gate
asks. What they are is **not yet workspace-scoped**, against the mandatory ruling that every page is
scoped to the selected workspace. The route now carries a `repoId` that these two screens do not
read. That belongs to whoever owns those screens; it is recorded here because a walk is where it
became visible, and Detectors' own subtitle already promises the scope its data does not have.

**A trap worth recording for the next walker:** `EVAL_CONSOLE` with a trailing slash produces

indistinguishable from a stale route, and it briefly convinced me four working screens were broken.
Pass the origin without a trailing slash.
