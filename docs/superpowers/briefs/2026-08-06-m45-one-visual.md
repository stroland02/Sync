## What the visual may not do

- **No composite score, health figure, traffic light, green dot, liveness pulse or count-up.** A
  chart is exactly where these arrive. A gauge needs a fixed maximum and a ratio; a status dot needs
  a stored state transition inside one closed lifecycle. Our data has neither, and `CLAUDE.md`
  carries why.
- **Absence is not zero.** A series with no data must not render as a value at the axis origin.
  Distinguish never-measured from measured-and-empty, on the chart, in words if necessary.
- **Colour is never the only channel.** A series a reader must distinguish by hue alone fails the
  same rule a status colour does.
- **No motion.** M4.5-W143 is auditing the three animations that exist; do not add a fourth. An
  entry transition on a chart is decoration, and `echarts`' defaults will give you one unless you
  turn it off.
- Nothing under `docs/superpowers/references/screenshots/` is opened.

## Constraints that will bite

- `echarts` is lazy-loaded into its own chunk so it never lands in the initial bundle — see
  `corpus-summary.tsx` and the `chunkSizeWarningLimit` comment in `vite.config.ts`. Keep it that
  way; a chart on the fleet index that pulls echarts into the first paint is a regression nobody
  will attribute to you.
- `components/charts/echart.tsx` reads `@theme` tokens through `getComputedStyle`, which is what
  makes a chart follow the token contract rather than hardcoding hexes. Use it.
- Logic with a wrong answer — bucketing, thresholds, what counts as a series — lives in Python and
  is tested there. The console formats and renders. `npm test` (`vitest`, landed as M4-W153) is for
  rendering-side classification.
